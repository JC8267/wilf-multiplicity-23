**Abstract:**

We give a lower bound for the Wilf number of a numerical semigroup in terms of a finite downset of Apery normal forms and the residues of its minimal generators. The bound is a weighted longest-path certificate and is uniform in the generator heights. A discrete summation identity and a carry estimate yield the shape criterion $W(S)\ge R_0(B)-D(B)$. Two independent exhaustive enumerations, each rerun in full, verify this criterion on every required cyclic tile of multiplicities $20$ through $23$, with one exception at multiplicity $21$. That exception is certified directly at all maximal targets in its unit orbit. Combining these calculations with established cases proves Wilf's conjecture for all numerical semigroups of multiplicity at most $23$, without a bound on genus or conductor.
